# scrum framework and team charter
ROLE: Scrum practitioner/ Agile business analyst

# 1. Agile methodology and cadence to ensure rapid iteration, clear cross-functional communication and contionus alignment with executive stakeholders. This project followed the strict scrum framework.
* Sprint duration: 2 week sprints
* Core artifacts maintained: Product backlog, sprint backlog and increment.
* Relative goal: Deliver a fully interactive, mathematically validated data prototype to the executive steering committe withion a 3-sprint delivery cycle.

--

# 2. Scrum ceremonies and operatinoal discipline
As a Scrum practitioner, I established and facilitated the following core team rituals to maintain maximum sprint velcity:

** SprintPlanning: Held on day 1 of every sprint. The team pulled prioritized user stories from product backlog, broke them into technical tasks, estimated effort into fiboonaci story points,
and committed to unified sprint goal.
** Daily standup: A daily 15 min sync focused on three core operatinoal metrics:
1. What did we achieve yesterday to achieve sprint goal?
2. What will we focus on today?
3. Are there any technical or data impediments.

**Sprint review and demo: held on the final friday of sprint. We demonstrated working data increments directly to the product owner and business sponsors to gather instant feedback loops.

**Sprint retrospective: The teams safe space to inspect and adapt our processes. This ceremony directly drove our decision to refactor our backend architecture into sql server for Phase 2.

--

# 3. Definition of Done 
To ensure every product increment delivered met enterprise-grade engineering standards, a user story was only marked as "DONE" if it cleared the following validation gates:

1. Data integrity: No data duplication or row inflation occurs across relationships; grand totals balance prefectly balanced.
2. Tecnical standards: All DAX logic utilizes explicit measures rather than implicit measures rather than implicit drag and drop columns to protect evaluation context.
3. Peer review: The data model schema a peer review check for single direction filter flow.
